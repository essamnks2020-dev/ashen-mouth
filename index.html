Craft principles

What I refuse to ship.

No generic chrome

If you can swap the logo and it looks like every other SaaS, it’s dead on arrival.

Friction with agency

Pause can be the product — but you still write. Tools don’t steal your voice.

Feel before features

Steam, hiss, ember pulse, door damp — sensory proof that something happened.

Privacy as structure

Local-first where the words are intimate. No analytics SDK disguised as “insights.”
