# ASHEN MOUTH — design card

**ASHEN MOUTH** is a playable first-person horror piece set in a coastal radio-monastery stacked into basalt. A bound “listening saint” learned human speech from decades of prayers and distress calls; it hunts sound, not sight. Your microphone is both threat and key.

**What makes it different:** stealth is silence, not darkness. Speaking is a mechanic — whispering the saint’s bound name (ORTHAEL, found in mural, ledger, and cracked vinyl) can pin or bind it; shouting your own name is suicide. The saint is a walking organ of stone (stacked mouths, brass radio-halo, phonograph-horn hands) with authored states: patrol, listen, hunt, inhale-pull, echo, pin.
